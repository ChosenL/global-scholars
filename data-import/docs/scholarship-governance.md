# Scholarship source governance

Scholarships publish only from the administering university, government, or provider. Search engines, aggregators, rankings, blogs, social media, and AI summaries are discovery-only and cannot independently support a record.

Every published award requires an exact name and provider, authoritative URL, stable source ID or deterministic exact key, retrieval timestamp, evidence checksum, mapping version, explicit international-eligibility state, verification status, and last-verified timestamp. `unspecified` never means eligible. Missing amounts, deadlines, renewability, restrictions, or requirements remain null or absent.

`current` means the authoritative source supports the represented award period at verification time. `future` is published only as non-active. `expired` is non-active. `unknown` is quarantined and cannot publish active. Evidence older than the configured verification window requires review. Renamed or ambiguous awards and Tier-2-only candidates are quarantined; fuzzy matching is prohibited.
